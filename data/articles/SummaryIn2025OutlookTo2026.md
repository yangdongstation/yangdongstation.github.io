The afterglow is like a cup of orange water, warmed just right and lightly poured into the wind chimes by the window. Yes, that self which previously existed only in daydreams... I'd never hidden my inclinations, but... I'd always been intimidated by countless pressures, too scared to take a single step forward. I hated the me who kept retreating... Yet it wasn't until there was nowhere left to retreat that I finally decided to move forward.

I wanted to grow stronger, to work on compilers, to run mirror stations, to become a very capable cat—an outstanding cat in my age group! So I picked up the ideapad I'd bought for 200 yuan... and began writing code line by line.

Buying servers, asking teachers for retired hard drives, recruiting people—step by step, I tried to bootstrap my own community... Thankfully... I've never lacked people by my side. There's always someone standing with me. They may not share my workload... but someone will definitely reach out when I'm lost and pull me back up.

Even when I've gone astray, those around me never scold me or say how terrible I am; she, they only reach out to pull me up, then draw me into their embrace... "It's okay, cat. You've worked hard enough. You've learned plenty."

This year I met many incredibly capable friends, reliable teammates, and amazing mentors. My friends span every corner of the world, every profession and industry—so wonderful, cat is so happy!

But... more often...
